LinePage

Fourteen numbers from 1 to 10. Paste one in to reopen that exact poem — it also lives in this page's web address, so the link you share is the poem you saw.

The form is Raymond Queneau's. The verses are not: these are ten original sonnets written for this machine, rhyming ABAB ABAB CCD EED so that every one of the hundred trillion combinations scans and rhymes. Reading all of them aloud, at one minute each, takes a little over 190 million years.
